Admission Information

We welcome students who are ready to learn, grow and become responsible members of society.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed admission form
  • Recent passport photograph
  • Previous school result
  • Birth certificate
  • Entrance examination

Admission Process

1. Obtain Form

Get and complete the school admission form.

2. Submit Documents

Submit the required documents to the school.

3. Entrance Examination

Students take an entrance examination.

4. Admission

Successful students receive admission into the school.
